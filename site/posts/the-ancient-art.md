---
title: The Ancient Art of the Todo List
date: 2026-09-01T12:00:00Z
---

The todo list is older than the smartphone, the startup, and probably even the
standing desk. Roman generals had plans. Monks had schedules. Your ancestors
had a scrap of papyrus reminding them to buy olives.

The modern todo app is the same idea with better typography and fewer chances
of losing the list in a sandal. Its greatest feature is not intelligence. It
is the gentle external pressure of seeing a list of unfinished tasks stare back at you.

There is something reassuring about a list that asks for no quarterly review.
It only asks: what is the next small thing?
