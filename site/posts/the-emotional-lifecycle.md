---
title: The Emotional Lifecycle of a Todo
date: 2026-09-02T12:00:00Z
---

Every todo begins as a confident promise: *I will do this today.*

By lunch, it has become a negotiation: *I will do this today, unless today
contains unexpected emails.* By evening, it is a historical artefact with a
surprisingly strong claim to tomorrow.

Completion is the happy ending. Deletion is also acceptable. The mature todo
manager understands that not every task needs closure; some need the dignity
of being quietly removed after three weeks of avoidance.
