---
title: Why Small Tasks Matter
date: 2026-09-04T12:00:00Z
---

Small tasks are the atoms of a productive day. Reply to one message. Put the
mug in the dishwasher before it becomes a permanent piece of archaeology.

A todo app gives these tasks a place to land. That matters because the brain is
excellent at inventing concerns and less excellent at remembering which one it
invented while you were brushing your teeth.

The goal is not to fill every minute. It is to make the next useful action
visible, then get on with life.
